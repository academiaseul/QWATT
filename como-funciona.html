Del medidor a Stellar testnet, pieza por piezaFrom the meter to Stellar testnet, piece by piece

En palabras simples: dos medidores leen la misma instalación; unos filtros descartan lo imposible; las lecturas aceptadas forman lotes encadenados por su huella; un proponente publica cada lote y firma su anclaje; un verificador independiente vuelve a derivar todo desde lo publicado y solo entonces cofirma; Stellar testnet guarda la huella y el total. Cualquiera puede volver a comprobarlo.In plain words: two meters read the same installation; filters throw out the impossible; accepted readings form batches chained by their fingerprints; a proposer publishes each batch and signs its anchor; an independent verifier re-derives everything from what was published and only then co-signs; Stellar testnet keeps the fingerprint and the total. Anyone can check it again.
