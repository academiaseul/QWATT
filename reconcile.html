This page downloads the public evidence feed, recomputes each batch's SHA-256 in your browser, and compares it with what the anchoring account wrote on Stellar. It also reads that account's signers and thresholds from Horizon and checks the signatures on every anchoring transaction. It trusts nothing the QWATT team says; the only inputs are the published files and the public ledger.

Do it without this page

  1. Download any batch from <feed>/batches/<batch_id>.json.
  2. Remove its sha256 field, serialize with sorted keys and no whitespace, and SHA-256 the UTF-8 bytes.
  3. Open the anchoring transaction on stellar.expert: its qwatt.anchor.sha256 data entry must equal your hash.
  4. Check the transaction's two signatures against the signer keys in the run's faults.json (anchoring.signers) and on the account: proposer + verifier is normal, proposer + third signer is a stand-in, anything else is broken.
  5. Read the account at <horizon>/accounts/<account>: thresholds 2/2/3 (low/medium/high), master weight 0, exactly three signers of weight 1.

Signature checks need WebCrypto Ed25519 (a current browser: Chrome 137+, Firefox 129+, Safari 17+). Without it this page gives no verdict; run node anchor/reconcile-cli.js instead.
