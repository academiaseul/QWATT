## LinkedIn — English

Three days ago, QWATT was born at a Stellar hackathon.

Today it's a working energy-verification protocol: qwatt.org — live electricity market data, a real digital asset on the Stellar testnet, and a mining prototype that pays for verified solar generation instead of hashpower.

The idea is simple. Green energy has a proof problem: claims are cheap, certificates are slow, and double counting is everywhere. Meanwhile, the evidence — meter data — already exists. It just never leaves the building.

QWATT connects that evidence to a public ledger:

⚡ A panel generates a kWh
📊 A meter measures it
✅ The event is verified and priced at the real market spot
🔗 A record lands on-chain, auditable by anyone, forever

We call the philosophy The Clean Token: the green value of energy should flow from evidence, not declarations. One MWh, claimed once.

Everything on the site is labeled exactly what it is — testnet, prototype, or planned. No fake numbers, no invented partners. The chart on the homepage is the actual European power market, including the hours solar pushes prices below zero.

Built on Stellar. First physical pilot in design in Lo Barnechea, Chile.

1 QWATT = 1 MWh of attested clean generation. One MWh, claimed once.

If you work in renewables, ESG reporting, or energy data — I'd genuinely love your skepticism. And if you want to follow along, leave your email at qwatt.org — no newsletter, we write back directly.
